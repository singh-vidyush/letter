For You, Alankrutha

A little something from my heart

I don't even know where to start, because whenever I try to put everything I feel for you into words, somehow the words never feel enough. ❤️

There are so many little things about you that have slowly become my favourite parts of my day. Your eyes, for example… I don't know what it is about them, but I can genuinely get lost in them. 🥹✨

There's something so beautiful in the way you look at things, the way your eyes light up when you're excited about something, and even the way they say things without you having to say a single word. Sometimes I just want to look at you and forget everything else for a while. 🤍

And then there's your smile. That smile has this unfair ability to change my entire mood. 😭❤️ Even on an ordinary day, seeing you smile somehow makes everything feel a little better. I don't think you realise how beautiful you look when you're genuinely happy.

Your voice is another thing I absolutely adore. 🫶🏻 I could listen to you talk about the most random things and still somehow enjoy every second of it.

And your laugh… honestly, your laugh might be one of my favourite sounds in the world. 😂❤️ Especially when you laugh without holding yourself back. There's something so pure and contagious about it that I can't help but smile too.

But it's not just about how beautiful you are. It's the person you are that keeps pulling me closer to you. The way you care about people, the little things you notice, the way you check on someone, the way you make people feel comfortable and cared for… those things mean so much more to me than you probably realise. ❤️

I love your presence too. Sometimes you don't even have to do anything. Just having you around makes things feel different. There's a certain comfort in knowing that you're there. 🥹🤍

And somehow, even the most normal conversations with you become memories I want to keep.

And your cute childish habits… 😂❤️ I don't know whether I should laugh at them, tease you about them, or secretly find them adorable. Probably all three.

I love that side of you. The silly little things, the random expressions, the way you get excited about something, the tiny dramas, and those moments when you completely forget to act your age. 😭😂 Please never lose that part of yourself.

And I genuinely love your "you know what happened?" talks. 😂

The moment you say those words, I already know I'm about to hear some completely random story, some tiny incident that somehow became the most important thing in the universe for the next ten minutes.

And honestly? I love listening to every single one of them. ❤️ Even when I have no idea where the story is going, I still want to hear it because it's you telling me.

And the funny thing is… when I first met you, I genuinely thought you were not in my league. 😅

I looked at you and thought, "There's no way someone like her would ever choose someone like me."

You seemed so far above what I thought I could ever have.

But somewhere along the way, that thought changed.

I stopped thinking about whether you were "out of my league" and started thinking about how incredibly lucky I would be if I got to have you in my life. ❤️

Because now I realise that this isn't about leagues, comparisons, or whether I'm good enough on paper.

It's about how I feel when I'm with you.

And the truth is… I think I would be happiest with you. 🥹❤️

Not because you're perfect. Not because everything would always be easy. But because you're you.

And somehow, the more I know you, the more I want to know you. The more time I spend with you, the more I want there to be more moments, more conversations, more stupid jokes, more "you know what happened?" stories, more laughs, more memories… just more of us. 🫶🏻

I don't know exactly what the future looks like. But if I get to choose the person I want beside me while I figure it out, I know who I'd choose. ❤️

Nuvvu naa life lo oka beautiful part kaadu… naa life lo nenu choose cheskovalani anukune person. 🥹❤️

Nijanga cheppalante, ninnu kalisina tarvata naaku oka feeling vachindi — "idhe ammayi naa pakkana unte, nenu chala happy ga untanu" ani. ❤️

Nuvvu naa tho kalisi ee journey ni start cheyyalani anukuntunnava? 🥺❤️

Alankrutha… ❤️

Nenu ninnu chala ishtapadutunnanu. ❤️

Naa life lo naa person ga, naa happiness lo naa partner ga, naa silly moments anni share cheskune person ga… naatho untava? 🥺
